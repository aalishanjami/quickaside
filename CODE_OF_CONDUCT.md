# Code of conduct

This project is for anyone who wants a better way through their calls. Please keep it a place where people want to help.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good faith.
- Disagree about ideas, not people.
- Take feedback well, and give it in a way others can use.
- Make room for people who are new or who write in a second language.

## What we won't accept

- Harassment, threats or personal attacks.
- Insults or hateful comments about who someone is.
- Sexual language or images.
- Sharing someone's private details without their consent, including anything from a call.

## If something goes wrong

Use **Report content** on the comment in question, or contact the maintainer through [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/aalishanjami). Reports stay private.

The maintainer can edit or remove comments, and can block people from the project. This applies in this repo and anywhere you represent the project.
