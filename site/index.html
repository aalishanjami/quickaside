Answers while you're still on the call.

Quickaside is a side panel for your Mac. It transcribes your call as it happens, and when the other person asks something, an answer appears beside the call. Answers come from your own notes, through your own Claude Code.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aalishanjami/quickaside/main/scripts/install.sh | bash

Free and open source. For Macs with Apple Silicon. Needs a paid Claude plan. See all the steps.

Note takers write things up after the call. This helps you during it.

How it works

  1. They ask

    Quickaside transcribes both sides of the call on your Mac. It hears what your Mac plays, so no bot joins your call.

  2. It checks your notes

    Your own Claude Code reads the call, the client's brief and your notes folder when the question needs them.

  3. You answer

    The answer lands in large type, close to your webcam. A full answer usually takes about 6 seconds, and sometimes up to 15. It streams, so you start reading sooner.

Answers are suggestions. Read them before you say them.

What it does

Works with any call app
Zoom, Meet, Teams, Slack, WhatsApp. When a call starts, it offers to listen. It never starts by itself.
Says what it doesn't know
It's told to claim only what's in your notes and the call. In the picture, it won't quote results it doesn't have.
Keeps clients apart
Pick a project, and answers use that client's brief first.
Writes it up after
A summary, decisions, action items and a follow-up email, with every call searchable.
Works with your Claude
Ask about past calls from Claude Code or Claude Desktop, if you turn that on. It can read your calls, and can't change them.
Free and open source
No Quickaside account, no subscription and no server of ours. All you need is your Claude plan.

What leaves your Mac

Stays on your Mac

Your audio. Speech is transcribed here, and audio is never saved.

Your saved calls, notes and settings.

Logs, which hold counts and timings and never your words.

Goes to Anthropic

Through your own Claude Code login: the transcript, your questions, your profile, the project brief and any notes Claude reads to answer.

With Answer every question they ask on, which is the default, this happens during the call without you pressing anything.

Comes to us

Nothing. There is no server to collect it. No analytics, no tracking and no crash uploads.

Read the full privacy page

Tell people you're transcribing

Their words are transcribed and sent to Anthropic through your account, and some places require everyone's consent. The panel reminds you when a call starts, and has a line you can copy into the meeting chat. If someone asks you to stop, press Pause.

Install

What you need

  • A Mac with Apple Silicon, on macOS 14.2 or later.
  • A paid Claude plan. Quickaside runs on Claude Code, which needs a Pro, Max, Ultra, Team, Enterprise or Console account. The free plan doesn't include it. Each answer counts toward your plan's usage limits.
  • About 1 GB of disk space, most of it for the speech model.

  1. Install Claude Code and sign in

    Skip this if you already use it.

    curl -fsSL https://claude.ai/install.sh | bash

    Then open a new Terminal window, run claude and log in.

  2. Install Quickaside

    curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aalishanjami/quickaside/main/scripts/install.sh | bash

    It needs no password. It installs to ~/Applications, and checks the app's signature before it touches anything.

  3. Follow the setup window

    It checks Claude Code, downloads the speech model and tests your permissions. macOS asks for two, so allow both: the microphone for your side of the call, and system audio recording for theirs.

Why a Terminal line? This project isn't in Apple's paid developer program, so macOS would block a browser download. If you'd rather download it, get the zip from Releases, then allow it in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway. The app updates itself after that, and never during a call.

Good to know

  • It's for Macs only, for now.
  • It knows You and Them, but not which person on their side said what.
  • It's built for English.
  • Whisper sometimes invents a short line on a quiet line, like "Thank you." The app filters most of these out.
  • It runs on your own Claude login, for your own use. A teammate should run it on their own Mac with their own account.
