    Connect Claude Code.

    Checking

    Download the speech model.

    Speech recognition runs on this Mac, so this is a one-time download.

    You can pick a smaller, faster model later in Settings.

    Allow the mic and call audio.

    macOS asks once for each. The call audio test plays a short tone.

    MicrophoneFor your side of the call.
    Call audioFor the other side of the call.

    Point it at your notes.

    Answers can only claim what your notes say. You can skip this and add them later.
